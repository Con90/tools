How to measure
  • Chest / bust: around the fullest part, tape level under the arms.
  • Waist: around your natural waist, the narrowest point.
  • Hips: around the fullest part of your hips and seat.
  • Inside leg: from crotch to ankle bone, along the inner leg.
  • Shoulder: across the back, shoulder point to shoulder point.
  • Sleeve: from shoulder point, over a slightly bent elbow, to the wrist.
  • Foot length: stand on paper with your heel against a wall; mark your longest toe and measure. Use your longer foot, measured in the evening.
Measure over thin clothing; keep the tape snug, not tight.

Find your colours

Add a few photos of your face and the app will measure your skin, hair and eye colours, work out your seasonal colour type and show the palette that suits you.

Tips for good photos
  • Daylight, facing a window, is best. Avoid yellow indoor bulbs, direct sun and shade.
  • Face the camera, with your whole face and some hair visible.
  • No make-up or filters if you can, and a neutral top (white or grey) helps.
  • 3–5 photos taken at different times give a steadier result than one.
  • Holding a sheet of white paper in the shot lets you correct the lighting: open the photo and mark it as white.
